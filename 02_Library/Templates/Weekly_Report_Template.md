# Weekly Progress Email Report Template
Path: `02_Library/Templates/Weekly_Report_Template.md`

Use this template to generate the weekly status email updates for stakeholders. It is optimized for clean copy-pasting directly into rich-text email editors (such as Outlook, Gmail, or corporate webmail).

---

## 📋 Copy-Paste & Styling Rules (Must Follow!)

To ensure the copied email looks professional and polished in the mail client:
1. **NO BOLD MARKDOWN (`**`):** Do not use `**` bold syntax in the email body or lists. It carries unwanted HTML formats, causing the text to paste with weird backgrounds or styles.
2. **NO DIVIDER LINES (`───` or `---`):** Never use visual divider lines or horizontal rules between sections. In dark themes, webmail, or Outlook, these trigger ugly black preformatted boxes on the clipboard. Use empty lines for visual gaps instead.
3. **PUNCHY NOUN ENDINGS (Korean List Items):** Korean list items must not end in trailing verbs (such as `-습니다` or `-요`). Keep them extremely short and finish with clean nominalized nouns (e.g., `~ 구축`, `~ 확장`, `~ 추가`, `~ 시각화`, `~ 완료`).
4. **ETIQUETTE VERB HARMONY (Korean Intro & Outro):** Use the polite, formal `-습니다` ending *only* at the very beginning (greeting/intro) and the very end (closing/questions) of the email to convey respect.
5. **D-BRAIN TERMINOLOGY STANDARDS:**
   - **System Name:** Always refer to it as **"Layout Automation system"** or **"레이아웃 자동화 프로젝트"**. Never mention private developer code names.
   - **Client Neutrality:** Do not use the word "고객사" or "client". Use neutral phrases like "requirements provided via email" or "전달해주신 메일 요구사항".
   - **Objects:** We place **"buildings (건물)"**, not "equipment (장비)". Never use machinery/equipment terms in the layout context.
6. **SHORT SENTENCES:** Keep all sentences extremely brief, clear, and direct. Eliminate developer jargon or complex mathematical concepts.
